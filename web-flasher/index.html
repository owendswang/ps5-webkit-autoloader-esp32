From USB dongle to console host

After installing project firmware, connect your console to ESP32_PORTAL to open the host.

Connect the board
Follow the USB instructions for your selected model. Close any serial monitor that might be using the port.
Install and verify
Choose a project release or a local .bin with its write address. Click Flash firmware and choose the board’s serial port. Keep the dongle plugged in until verification finishes.
Connect your console
Plug the dongle into a USB port for power. Join ESP32_PORTAL with password 12345678. On PS5, open User’s Guide from Settings. On PS4, open the browser at http://192.168.4.1/. Check the release notes for supported console firmware.
